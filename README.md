# CC6104-Statistical-Thinking

This repository contains the assignments completed during the course **CC6104: Statistical Thinking**, taught by **Felipe Bravo M.** at the **Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, University of Chile**.
The course introduced the foundations of statistical thinking as a systematic approach to understanding real-world phenomena under uncertainty and making data-driven decisions. It covered descriptive data analysis, statistical programming in R, probability theory, and the main paradigms of statistical inference: frequentist and Bayesian. Advanced topics included model evaluation using information theory, graphical models, and multilevel models.

## Assignments

- **T1:** Estadística básica  
- **T2:** Frequentist Inference  
- **T3:** Frequentist Inference II  
- **T4:** Bayesian Inference  
- **T5:** Bayesian Inference Part II
